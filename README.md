# 💗 heardYou
### Every symptom deserves to be heard.
### A whole-body women's health education and symptom-pattern exploration platform.

**HeardYou** is a women's healthcare education and symptom-pattern exploration platform that helps women understand how health conditions may present differently, explore overlapping symptom patterns, and prepare for informed conversations with healthcare professionals.

Built for **recHERsion '26 — Women's Healthcare**, HeardYou aims to bridge the gap between experiencing symptoms, finding credible health information, and communicating concerns effectively.

> Understand the information. Explore the patterns. Prepare to be heard.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Women's health experiences can be complex. Symptoms may vary across individuals and life stages, overlap across multiple conditions, and be difficult to explain during a medical consultation.

Three major gaps contribute to this challenge:

- **Presentation Gap:** Medical descriptions may not fully communicate the range of ways conditions can present in women.
- **Communication Gap:** Explaining combinations of symptoms, their severity, and duration can be difficult during a short consultation.
- **Action Gap:** Scattered online information makes it harder to find credible guidance and prepare meaningful questions for a healthcare professional.

Women need more than another symptom tracker. They need accessible explanations, trustworthy sources, and support in communicating what they experience.

## 💡 Our Solution

HeardYou transforms scattered health information into a structured, understandable experience that supports informed healthcare conversations.

The platform follows three core stages:

- **KNOW — Understand:** Explore health conditions and compare textbook descriptions with documented presentations in women.
- **MATCH — Explore:** Select symptoms and examine overlapping patterns with transparent explanations of why they appear.
- **ACT — Prepare:** Review sourced warning signs, explore questions to discuss with a clinician, and prepare a structured symptom brief.

Our goal is not to replace medical professionals, but to help women become better informed and better prepared to discuss their health concerns.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📚 1. Condition Atlas
- Explore health conditions organized by body system.
- Compare textbook presentations with documented presentations in women.
- Search symptoms to discover associated conditions.
- Explore information relevant to different life stages.

### 🔎 2. Symptom Pattern Exploration
- Complete a structured symptom check-in.
- Record symptom severity and duration.
- Explore overlapping patterns through deterministic, weighted matching.
- Understand why a pattern appears through matched symptoms and transparent explanations.

### 🩺 3. Evidence-Informed Action Support
- Access published warning-sign information with source links.
- Explore questions to discuss with a healthcare professional.
- Review suggested tests and specialist information where supported by the available content.
- Prepare a downloadable symptom brief for a medical consultation.

### 🔐 4. Safety and Privacy
- No account or login required.
- Designed to avoid server-side storage of personal health information.
- Medical information linked to credible sources for verification.
- Warning-sign rules evaluated independently of symptom-pattern scores.
- No diagnostic claims or personal urgency scores.

## 🌟 What Makes HeardYou Different?

**Context Over Isolated Symptoms**

HeardYou helps users explore how symptoms may overlap across conditions and life stages rather than viewing every symptom in isolation.

**Explainability Over Black-Box Predictions**

The matching approach uses explicit rules and shows the symptoms behind each result. Users can understand why a pattern appeared rather than receiving an unexplained output.

**Preparation Over Self-Diagnosis**

HeardYou connects educational information with practical consultation preparation, helping users organize their experiences and questions for a healthcare professional.

**Safety Over False Reassurance**

The platform distinguishes published warning signs from exploratory symptom patterns. A missing match must never be interpreted as proof that nothing is wrong.

## 🛡️ Safety, Privacy and Responsible Use

HeardYou is a healthcare education prototype, not a diagnostic tool or a replacement for professional medical care.

- Symptom matches represent patterns worth discussing, not confirmed conditions.
- Matching scores are not probabilities of having a condition.
- Medical claims and warning-sign rules require verification against their cited sources.
- The absence of a warning-sign match does not mean that nothing is wrong.
- Personal health inputs should not be stored or logged on the server.
- Users should seek professional medical care when concerned. Possible emergency symptoms require prompt assessment and must not be dismissed because of an application result.

**Medical Disclaimer:** HeardYou provides educational information, not medical advice. It does not diagnose conditions or replace assessment by a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency in India, call 112.

## 🗺️ Future Roadmap

- **Clinical Review:** Work with qualified healthcare professionals to review medical content, matching rules, and warning-sign guidance.
- **Regional Language Support:** Make women's health education more accessible to users who prefer Indian regional languages.
- **Expanded Condition Coverage:** Include additional health conditions and life stages as reliable, verified content becomes available.

These improvements aim to make HeardYou more accessible, evidence-informed, and useful for healthcare conversations.
